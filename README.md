# Cool Air Hub

You are an expert full-stack software architect, UI/UX designer, and senior developer.

Build a premium, production-ready, responsive web application for a family-owned home appliance business named "Cool Air".

The business has been operating successfully for over four years in Kochi, Kerala, India.

The company has two divisions.

1. Home Appliance Repair & Service

2. Refurbished (Second-Hand) Home Appliance Sales

The owner has experienced technicians who repair and maintain all kinds of home appliances.

Services include

• Air Conditioner Service

• Refrigerator Repair

• Washing Machine Repair

• Microwave Repair

• Water Purifier Service

• Dishwasher Repair

• Installation

• Uninstallation

• Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)

The second division sells high-quality refurbished home appliances including

• Refrigerators

• Washing Machines

• Air Conditioners

• Microwaves

• Water Purifiers

• Mixers

• Induction Stoves

• Other household appliances

The objective is to build a modern digital platform that can later grow into Kerala's biggest appliance service and spare parts business.
